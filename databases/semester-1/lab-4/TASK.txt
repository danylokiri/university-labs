Practical Work 4 - CASE Tools for Business-Process Modelling: DFD and IDEF3 Methodologies

Topic: CASE tools for modelling information-system business processes, DFD and IDEF3 methodologies.
Goal: Gain experience in creating DFD and IDEF3 business-process models using the CASE tool BPwin.

Task:
Task 1. Using the subject assigned by the instructor, build the model described in the
theoretical part, using your own group, full name and the company name given by the variant
in the model names. In BPwin, create a functional model of an information system that meets
these requirements:
 - methodology: DFD;
 - at least 3 levels of detail;
 - at least 4 diagrams, one of which is in IDEF3.
Task 2. Analyse the correspondence between the model of the automated processes and the
functional model of the information system, and explain any discrepancies found.

Notes: Subject area of the variant: Farm. The work builds on the previous practical work (IDEF0 model).
Tool: BPwin.
