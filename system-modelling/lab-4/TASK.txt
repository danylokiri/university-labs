System Modelling, Practical 4: Control Criteria of Complex Network Systems

Topic: Instantaneous and integral quality and efficiency criteria of the operation of a network system.
Goal: Calculate the quality and efficiency criteria of the operation of a complex network system for given pressures at the nodes and flow of the active source.

Task:
4a. Instantaneous criteria. According to the given variant of the graph structure and the given values of pressure at the network nodes and of flow at the outputs of active sources, calculate the values of the quality and efficiency criteria of its operation for a fixed moment of time k.
4b. Integral criteria. Two discrete moments of time are considered, for example a morning moment T1 and an evening moment T2. For the given variant of the graph structure and the given pressures at the nodes and flows at the outputs of active sources, calculate the values of the quality and efficiency criteria with respect to each node with the consumers connected to it for the whole time interval [T1, T2]. Calculate the values of the convolutions of the quality and efficiency criteria for the whole interval [T1, T2].

Notes: Network: the 7-node graph of Practical 3 with the active source at node 1; required (dictating) pressure H = 60 m.
  Pressures at nodes 1..7 at T1: 65, 64, 61, 58, 61, 54, 58 m; at T2: 66, 63, 61, 59, 62, 56, 59 m.
  Source flow Q1: 2700 at T1, 2800 at T2.
