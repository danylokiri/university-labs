Lab 1 - Analysis of the blockchain of the Bitcoin cryptocurrency platform

Topic: Analysis of the Bitcoin blockchain
Goal: Get practical familiarity with the Bitcoin network using resources that allow browsing the blockchains of cryptocurrency platforms (blockchain explorers).

Task:
1. Using a Bitcoin blockchain explorer, identify the main fields of a block (block height, timestamp, block hash, previous block hash, nonce, difficulty, transactions, version, block weight) and describe each of them.
2. Investigate 12 blocks, each generated in a different month of the same year, and 5 blocks generated in different years.
3. Build graphs of the change of mining difficulty based on the collected data and describe the observed trend (Bitcoin adjusts difficulty every 2016 blocks).
4. Answer the control questions: how a blockchain differs from a database; what a blockchain is; how mining works in Bitcoin (Proof-of-Work); the structure of a Bitcoin block and the role of the Merkle root; what mining difficulty is and how it is adjusted.

Notes: Data source: a public Bitcoin blockchain explorer. The task statement in the source report is only partially present; the list above is reconstructed from the stated goal and the described work.
