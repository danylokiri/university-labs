Credit work - Blockchain technology and cryptocurrency operating platforms

Topic: Theory of blockchain technology and cryptocurrency platforms (written credit work, 4 theory questions, 10 points each)
Goal: Demonstrate understanding of consensus protocols, the Bitcoin and Ethereum platforms and third-generation cryptocurrencies.

Task (answer the following questions in writing):
1. The concept of a consensus protocol and its main functions in decentralized systems.
2. Describe the general architecture and the principles of interaction in the Bitcoin network.
3. Transactions and smart contracts in Ethereum and their properties. The structure of an account. The difference between the account model and the UTXO model.
4. Properties of third-generation cryptocurrencies. The main principles of how the Polkadot platform works.

Notes: Written work; there is no programming part.
